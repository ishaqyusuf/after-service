## Selection

Start with five operators across repair shops, installers, and local contractors.
Prefer an owner or coordinator with 1–10 staff, several completed jobs each week,
and a specific post-job check-in that is currently handled through memory,
notes, a calendar, or a spreadsheet. Prioritize people the founder can observe
and follow up with. Expand toward 10–20 workspaces after early friction is fixed.

Use the table to identify candidates; do not treat the segment slots as real
leads. Add names only after choosing actual people. Keep personal phone/email
in the existing contact system, and use its reference here.

## Activation Tracker

Add one row per real workspace after onboarding:

| Prospect ID | Workspace ID | Onboarded date | First customer/job/follow-up | First real contact logged | Repeated without help | Day 7 return observed | Main friction / next check-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A sample walkthrough is not real customer activation. Record contact only after
it happens and distinguish phone contact logging from email provider delivery.
If the date or outcome is unknown, leave it unknown rather than inferring it
from a page visit or button click.

## Pilot Evidence Targets

- First cohort: five workspaces onboarded; three complete one real follow-up.
- Habit: five to eight teams use the product weekly for at least three weeks.
- Paid intent: at least three operators explicitly say they would pay.
- All current counts remain unrecorded until supported by real evidence.
