# Founder-Led Beta Onboarding

Prepared: 2026-10-18
Goal: A service operator can log a completed job and schedule a follow-up in
under 20 minutes, then repeat it without help.

## Before The Session

- Confirm the operator fits the pilot: repair shop, installer, contractor, or
  field team with 1–10 staff and recurring post-job check-ins.
- Agree a meeting time and a reply channel; record both in the recruiting tracker.
- Share the signup link and explain Free Beta/no credit card required.
- Google and email/password sign-in passed production acceptance. In-app email
  delivery still awaits Trigger credits; use manual phone contact logging.
- Ask the operator to choose one job they may enter. Start with an obviously
  labelled sample if they prefer. Do not copy customer data into research notes.
- The operator enters their own password. Do not request or record it.

## Session Script

| Minutes | Action | Founder prompt | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Understand current practice | “Walk me through the last job you followed up. What reminded you?” | Current tool, trigger, and one actual friction point |
| 3–6 | Sign up and create workspace | “Use your business name and choose the follow-up delay that fits your work.” | Signup/onboarding completed, workspace ID if available |
| 6–9 | Add one customer | “Add only the details you need to find this customer later.” | Customer saved; operator can find it again |
| 9–12 | Record a completed job | “What work was done, and when was it finished?” | Job saved against the correct customer |
| 12–15 | Schedule a phone follow-up | “When should someone check in, and what would they ask?” | Follow-up appears in the expected board group; template/draft reviewed |
| 15–18 | Explain contact, reply, and closure | “After you call through your normal process, record it here. What would you do if they report an issue?” | Operator can describe record contact → reply → close/reschedule |
| 18–20 | Repeat and agree next step | “Without my help, find that job and schedule or reschedule its check-in.” | Independent completion or exact point where help was needed |

Do not log a real call as sent unless the call happened. For a sample record,
clearly label the contact as a demonstration and close it afterwards. Do not
close a real unresolved issue merely to complete the walkthrough.

## Operator Quick Start

1. Sign in at https://dash.afterservice.app/sign-in with Google or email and password.
2. Add the customer, then record the completed job in Jobs.
3. Create a phone follow-up from the job and check its date and draft.
4. Review the Follow-ups board when starting your day.
5. Contact the customer through your normal phone process, then log the contact.
6. Record their reply. Close resolved work or reschedule the next action.
7. Reply to your founder onboarding conversation when anything is confusing.

A missed item means the due time passed without contact being logged. Daily
housekeeping may update it during the morning; the board remains the source
for what needs attention. This is not an automated customer reminder.

## Session Exit Checklist

- [ ] Operator created their own account and workspace.
- [ ] Customer and completed job are linked correctly.
- [ ] Follow-up is scheduled and visible.
- [ ] Operator repeated the key action without coaching.
- [ ] Sample records are labelled/closed; genuine outstanding work stays open.
- [ ] Operator knows the feedback reply channel and next check-in date.
- [ ] Record observed facts, assistance required, and the top friction point.

## Follow-Up Prompts For The Founder

Send these manually only after reviewing the operator context:

- Day 2: “Have you added another completed job? Where did you get stuck?”
- Day 7: “Which follow-ups did you complete? What still needed your old process?”
- Week 3: “What made you return, or what stopped you? What would you miss if
  afterservice disappeared?”

These are draft prompts, not scheduled automations or messages already sent.
