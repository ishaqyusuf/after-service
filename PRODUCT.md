# afterservice

After-service follow-up software for local operators. The brand is afterservice;
the public site is afterservice.app and the dashboard is dash.afterservice.app.

Operators record customers and completed jobs, schedule a dated check-in, contact
the customer through their usual channel, then log contact, reply and outcome.
The five board stages are Due today, Upcoming, Waiting, Replied and Closed.
The interface must stay clear for non-technical owners and work on phones.

Product truth lives in `brain/BRAIN.md`, `brain/system/overview.md` and current
feature documentation. Free/Business limits and fixed regional monthly prices
come from `packages/plans`; country resolution and checkout remain server-owned.
Assistant behavior follows the current implementation and feature gates.
Do not infer availability from historical Workshop copy.
